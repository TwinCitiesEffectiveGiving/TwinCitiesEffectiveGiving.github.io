About us

A giving circle with a bias toward evidence

Twin Cities Effective Giving is a local group for anyone who wants their donations to do as much good as possible. Together we explore the research, debate tough questions, and pool our giving toward the causes where it helps most.

  • Monthly meetups in the Twin Cities metro
  • No experience with philanthropy research required
  • Open to every giving level — your voice counts
